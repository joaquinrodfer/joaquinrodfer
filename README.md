## 👋 About me

**Computer Engineer** at the **Universidad de Jaén**, about to start a **Master's in Data Science & AI at Evolve** this October. I turn messy, complex data into practical solutions through analysis, automation and software engineering.

I've worked as an **AI & Automation Researcher**, running and evaluating Computer Vision / Deep Learning models (YOLO and others) over datasets of millions of satellite images, building pipelines to automate repetitive workflows and documenting the research along the way. I also connect relational databases with BI tools (Power BI, KNIME) to turn raw tables into dashboards people actually use for decisions.

Outside of "official" work, I like owning my own infrastructure — I run a **Homelab on Proxmox** — and I build side projects end-to-end, from the database up to the UI.
